Voice rules

  • Short. Technical. In-thread.
  • No calendar link. No “quick call.”
  • One CTA: a live Stripe URL or a subject line on Contact.
  • Do not attach Field Practice files to a Signal buyer, or the pack to an unpaid thread.

What will not be built from this page

  • A purchased sequencer, warm-up pool, or scraped-list campaign.
  • A 5-email “education drip” that restates the landing page.
  • Any sequence that assigns Jeff a slot.
  • Catalog, merch, or Patreon drips on this host.

Records of who was mailed live on CRM. Public posts live on Outbound.
